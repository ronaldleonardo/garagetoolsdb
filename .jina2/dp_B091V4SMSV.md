## About this item

*    Versatile Fit Options: Includes 10 different oil filter cap sizes compatible with a wide range of vehicles, including BMW, Ford, Chevy, and Toyota, making this oil filter cap wrench set an essential tool for any oil change kit. 
*    Non-Slip Performance: Die-formed socket grips ensure the oil filter socket stays securely in place during removal, preventing slippage and reducing the risk of damage, even when working with oil-covered filters. 
*    Rust-Resistant Coating: Each oil filter socket in this set is painted to resist rust, ensuring long-lasting durability and reliable performance in tough automotive environments, making it ideal for frequent use. 
*    3/8" Ratchet Compatibility: Designed to fit standard 3/8" ratchets and extensions, this oil filter wrench set allows for quick and efficient oil filter removal, simplifying the oil change process for both professionals and DIY enthusiasts. 
*    Protects Oil Filters: This oil filter cap removal tool is engineered to remove filters without crushing or damaging them, ensuring a secure fit and easy removal every time, essential for maintaining vehicle integrity.
