## Product Description

The Performance Tool W173C is the perfect solution for tough jobs, thanks to its heavy-duty construction featuring a durable chrome plated steel bar. Its no-slip strap securely grips filters up to 6 inches in diameter, preventing slippage and damage, and can be turned with a 1/2 inch ratchet for easy use and time-saving efficiency. This versatile tool can be used on a wide range of filters, making it a must-have addition to any mechanic or DIY enthusiast's toolbox.
